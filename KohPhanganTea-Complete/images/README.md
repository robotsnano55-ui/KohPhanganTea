# Tea Images

Place your tea product images here:

- `gabo-tea.jpg` - Image of Gabo tea
- `puer-tea.jpg` - Image of Puer tea  
- `red-tea.jpg` - Image of Red tea

Recommended image specifications:
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Size: 800x800px (square)
- File size: Under 500KB for fast loading
- High quality, well-lit photos showing the tea

You can use:
- Your own product photos
- Stock photos from Unsplash, Pexels, or Pixabay
- AI-generated images from Midjourney or DALL-E
